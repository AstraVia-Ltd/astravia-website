Case studies

Proof in practice

A few examples of where AstraVia has turned operational problems and product ideas into working systems.

Real-time performance app for rowing athletes and coaches

RowCoach

Problem

Rowing performance data is often locked behind expensive specialist hardware or fragmented across separate tools.

What we built

A mobile app combining GPS speed and split tracking, accelerometer-based stroke-rate detection, heart-rate integration, cloud session storage, CSV export and live data sharing.

Impact

Coaches and athletes get useful real-time training insight through a simpler, lower-cost system.

Relevant capability: Mobile app development, sensor data, live data sharing, cloud infrastructure, user-focused interface design.

Safeguarding, SEND & medical information system

WrapAroundCare4u

Problem

Critical information for nearly 3,000 children needed to be easier to manage, update and access across the organisation.

What we built

A custom frontend and backend system supported by easy-to-use spreadsheet workflows, giving staff a clearer way to manage SEND, safeguarding and medical information.

Impact

Reduced manual admin, improved visibility, and made a previously time-heavy process much easier to run.

Product business, operations & sales automation

Kompact9

Problem

A small product business needed to expand retail outreach without spending days manually searching for suitable stockists.

What we built

A web-scraping and spreadsheet-integrated research tool that searched target areas, identified relevant pet shops, and returned available contact details and locations.

Impact

Turned manual research into a repeatable sales process and saved hundreds of hours of work.

Wider experience: Kompact9 also demonstrates end-to-end product experience — concept, prototyping, manufacturing, logistics, retail, sales and market validation.
