How we work

Find the problem first. Then design and build the fix.

01

Find the friction

We start with the people who actually do the work and follow the process end to end. Then we pinpoint the bottleneck, the place where efficiency slows now, and measure the business impact, errors and turnaround time.

02

Design the workflow

How it should run, who needs to see what, which tools it needs and what a person does and doesn't need to spend time doing. We try to use the tools you already have, and where needed, add or build new ones where they earn their place.

03

Build and implement

We connect your existing systems, build what's missing and put it into real use with your team, with a documented handover so everyone knows how it works.

04

Iterate and maintain

Once it's live we watch how it performs, fix what drifts and improve it around what actually happens day to day, with a monthly review of the hours saved.

Start with one workflow, prove it works, then move on to the next bottleneck. You have one point of contact from the first call right through to keeping it running, so there's no hand-off between an adviser and a developer.

Typical workflows we automate

  • Enquiries and quotes logged, answered and followed up without anyone copying details across
  • Orders, job sheets and invoices moving between systems without re-keying
  • Customer questions answered straight away, with the tricky ones passed to a person alongside a draft reply
  • Chasing documents, approvals and payments automatically
  • Reporting that pulls the weekly numbers together and sends them out on its own
  • Scattered information from spreadsheets, inboxes and systems brought into one place you can actually use

In practice

What it looks like when it's working

Automated reporting workflow

WrapAroundCare4U

Childcare provider, 15 school settings

The problem

Fifteen settings each send reports to head office. They were copied by hand into documents, and keeping on top of what needed attention was slow.

What we did

An automated report management workflow between their services, built for quality control inside the Google Workspace they already use. Managers fill in a short form; every report lands in one leadership dashboard, critical items are flagged and reminders go out automatically. We also built their SEND, safeguarding and medical information system for nearly 3,000 children.

The result

Around a day a week of admin saved, better visibility, and a time heavy process that is now much easier to run.

"The time saved means I have the capacity to focus more on strategy and growing the business."

Karen, Director, WrapAroundCare4U

Automated outreach and customer service

Kompact9

Pet product business, 30,000+ units sold

The problem

Expanding retail outreach meant days of manual searching for suitable stockists, and customer queries were eating into the team's time.

What we did

An automated wholesale research and outreach workflow: it searches target areas, identifies relevant pet shops and returns their contact details, so research became a repeatable sales process. Then a customer service agent that answers 98% of queries on its own; the other 2% reach the customer service manager with a draft reply ready to check.

The result

Hundreds of hours of work saved, and outreach that runs every week without starting from a blank search.

"Finding new stockists used to be days of searching. Now the list is waiting for us, and the agent handles almost everything before it reaches a person."

Alex, Director, Kompact9

Custom app build

A fair question

Why not just use off the shelf software?

Sometimes you should. If an existing tool solves the problem well, that's usually the fastest, most cost effective answer. But many organisations reach a point where standard tools nearly work, while staff are still copying data, managing exceptions manually or building awkward workarounds. AstraVia helps in that gap: improving the process, connecting the tools, and building custom software only where it genuinely adds value. The same logic applies to AI: sometimes an agent is exactly the right tool for a repetitive task, sometimes it's overkill for what's really a two step fix. We help you tell the difference, rather than reaching for it by default.
